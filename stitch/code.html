Simple ingredients . Unexpected memories

At Crousty Grill, we discard superfluous garnishes in pursuit of raw elemental clarity. Every morning begins with wood collection: seasoned white oak and wild maritime rosemary twigs from certified southern forestry estates.

Sourced directly from small-scale coastal Mediterranean fishermen and high-altitude Pyrenean ranches, each cut is dry-aged on site in our glass temperature cellar before meeting custom-welded steel grates over open embers.
