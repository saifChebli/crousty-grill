[Sec 02 // Philosophy & fire]

[Provenance archive]

Where it starts

Simple ingredients, unexpected memories

At Crousty Grill we skip the needless garnish and let fire do the work. Every morning starts with the wood: seasoned white oak and wild rosemary from certified forests in the south of France.

Our fish comes straight from small Mediterranean boats and our meat from high Pyrenean farms. Each cut is dry-aged on site in our glass cellar before it meets the steel grates over open embers.
